World Bank Group, Climate Change Division Short Term Temporary (STT) Nov 2022 to Dec 2023 Delivered as my MPP capstone, December 2023

What drives deforestation in the Brazilian Amazon

The Climate Change team's starting question was whether hydropower and biomass were driving forest loss in the Amazon. I began there and widened the study to commodity markets, protected areas, and settlement. The work set 20 years of satellite forest-loss data against commodity prices, Brazilian export volumes, land designations, and population growth.

What the data showed

  1. Cumulative loss reached roughly 390,000 km² by 2020, about 10% of the 4 million km² of forest standing in 2000. Pará and Mato Grosso had the most volatile year-to-year clearing.
  2. Annual loss fell through 2003 to 2010, a period that includes the 2006 Soy Moratorium, then spiked in 2016.
  3. Prices tracked export volumes and values for all three commodities, most closely for beef and wood (table below).
  4. Indigenous lands held their forest better than any other land designation. Some conservation units showed clearing inside their boundaries.

Nuentero Inc Founder May 2025 to present Delaware C corporation, Washington, DC

Solar loan models and the investor platform behind them

Nuentero finances renewable energy in the U.S. and in developing markets. Its first loan portfolio, in Colombia, was funded by 7 investors across 6 projects in 4 cities. I built the financial models, ran the borrower and installer diligence, and wrote the software investors use to follow their capital.

  • Loan portfolio models for residential, commercial, and industrial solar: NPV, IRR, payback, and sensitivity cases.
  • A USD-COP hedging model that shows investors their currency exposure and what a hedge costs them.
  • Diligence on 10+ residential borrowers: credit and ability to pay, property ownership, roof, breaker panel, and interconnection checks, and vetting of the developers and installers serving them.
  • The investor platform: React front end, FastAPI and PostgreSQL back end, Auth0 sign-in, and separate views for investors, developers, and white-label sponsors, in English and Spanish.

A supplier register for Colombia's offshore wind supply chain

MarViento is a directory of the companies that can serve Colombia's offshore wind build-out. I modeled it on Oceantic Network's Supply Chain Connect, the U.S. database I researched companies for as an intern in 2022, and designed the taxonomy, the supplier registration flow, and the admin approval step.

106companies mapped
187categories of services, products, and materials
4languages: Spanish, English, Portuguese, Chinese

I presented it to EEMa Colombia's leadership as a candidate national supplier register. Since September 2026 I have sat on EEMa's Supply Chain Roundtable, one of six groups drafting a civil-society offshore wind strategy for Colombia covering 2027 to 2037.
